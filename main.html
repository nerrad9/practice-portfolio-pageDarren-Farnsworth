About Me

I always have an idea to share. Whether it's a good idea or a bad idea depends on how open minded you are. From puzzle books as a child to word problems in math class, I've always enjoyed coming up with unique solutions to challenging problems. I learned programming as it offered many unique tools to solve my day to day problems. After learning everything I could I began to share that knowledge with others. Having experience in training new hires working food service thoughout high school and college, I became a private tutor, teaching programming to children of all ages. I worked there for 6 years, in that time I became the head coach and took ever running the operation when my boss went on vacations. I feel like I learned a lot more than I taught doing that job. It not only forced me to dive head first into new languages and libraries, but also helped reinforce my leadership skills. Now I feel confident that I have the skills needed to lead, teach, and create just about anything I can think of, whether it's a bad idea or not.

Things I've wasted time on

  • PokeRanker

    PokeRanker is my attempt to rank all 1025 Pokemon using the Elo Ranking System. By showing a grid of 25 psuedo randomly selected Pokemon, It is able to rapidly calculate a fairly accurate Elo score and ranking for almost every Pokemon in only ~67 trials. (That's not even a 67 joke, I plugged the numbers in and that's what the calculator gave me)

  • Are You Smarter Than a 5th Grader

    I unfortunately know some people who would fail this quiz. This simple quiz app only asks you to answer questions for 5th graders. How well do you actually remember 5th grade? Do you know what body part filters and makes new blood? Do you honestly remember what classifies an animal as a mammal? If you think you could take a 5th grader on in a scholar olympiad, then try it out!

  • Taco Bell Webscraper(Deprecated)

    This simple python script crawls every page of the Taco Bell website, finds all images of food, then downloads and sorts all media into folders based on the page hierarchy of the Taco Bell website. It no longer works because ai training data webcrawlers and cloudflare are in a never ending cat and mouse arms race and everyone else who just wants to do dumb stuff is caught in the crossfire. RIP casual webscraping.
